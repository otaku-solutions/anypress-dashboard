title: Answering common objections
---

Listen first, ask one question back, then answer in a sentence or two. Each group's own objections are in its playbook under Who To Sell To.

## "It's too expensive."

**Topic:** Price
**Ask back:** "What do you pay today, all in? Hosting, the site builder, bookings, email, forms, the designer who fixes things."
**Then say:** One monthly price usually replaces three or four bills. If it's still too much, start on a smaller plan and move up when you grow.

## "I can do it myself with a free builder."

**Topic:** Price
**Ask back:** "How many hours a month do you spend on it, and what's that time worth?"
**Then say:** You can, and many people do. I look after updates, security, backups and support, and it's a real WordPress site you own and can move.

## "I already have a website."

**Topic:** Timing
**Ask back:** "When was it last updated? Does it take bookings or payments? How does it look on a phone?"
**Then say:** Let me run a free website check. {{brand}} sends a short report you can keep, whatever you decide.

## "I need to think about it."

**Topic:** Timing
**Ask back:** "What do you need to think through?"
**Then say:** Answer that one thing. Then agree a date to talk again, and send a one-page summary they can share.

## "I'm locked into a contract."

**Topic:** Switching
**Ask back:** "When does it end?"
**Then say:** I'll put a reminder in for a month before, and have the new site ready so you can switch the day the old one ends.

## "What if I want to leave?"

**Topic:** Switching
**Ask back:** "What would make you want to leave?"
**Then say:** Your content is yours. You can export the site and move it, and there's no long contract.

## "Is it secure?"

**Topic:** Trust
**Ask back:** "Have you had a problem before?"
**Then say:** Every site runs on its own, with its own database, nightly backups, updates handled for you and a free security certificate.

## "Who do I call when something breaks?"

**Topic:** Trust
**Ask back:** "How fast do you need an answer?"
**Then say:** Me. You reach me at {{support}}, and I'll reply within the time I promise you.

## "I've been burned by a web designer before."

**Topic:** Trust
**Ask back:** "What went wrong last time?"
**Then say:** Look at the live demo, and I'll set your site up before you pay, so you see exactly what you get. Prices are fixed and written down.
