title: The 15-minute call
---

A 15-minute call to find out whether you can help, show the demo and agree a next step. Book a second call if they want more.

## Before the call

- Run the website check on their current site and have the report open.
- Open the demo for their group: {{demo}}.
- Read their Google listing and social pages: what they sell, their reviews, their busiest channel.

## Open (1 minute)

"Thanks for making time, {{prospect}}. I've got 15 minutes blocked. I'd like to ask a few questions about {{business}}, show you a quick example, and if it looks useful we can agree what happens next. Sound good?"

- Say how long the call will take.
- Say what you'll cover.

Keep it short. The point is to get them talking.

## Ask (5 minutes)

"How do customers find you today?"

- "How do customers find you today?"
- "What does your website do for you right now? Bookings, sales, enquiries?"
- "What tools do you pay for to run the business online?"
- "Who updates the site, and how long does it take?"
- "If you could fix one thing about it, what would it be?"

Ask, then listen. Write down their words; you'll use them in the follow-up.

## Show (5 minutes)

"You said bookings come in by phone. Here's what it looks like when people book online."

- Share your screen with the demo for their group.
- Show only the three things from their group's playbook that match what they said.
- Use their words, not feature names.

Three things they asked about beat ten things they didn't.

## Price (2 minutes)

"For {{business}}, I'd suggest the {{plan}} plan. It's {{price}} a month, and that includes hosting, updates, backups and support from me."

- Name one plan, not a menu.
- Say the price once.
- Stop talking and let them answer.

The quiet after the price is normal. Wait for their answer.

## Next step (2 minutes)

"Shall I set up your site with your logo and details so you can see it for real?"

- Or: "I'll send a summary by email today. Can we talk again on {{day}}?"
- Or, if they're ready: "I'll send you the sign-up link now: {{shop}}."

Leave with one agreed next step and a date.

## After the call

- Send the follow-up email within the hour, using their words and the next step you agreed.
- Add them to Your Prospects with their group and stage.
